What Postern is

Postern passes a password, text, link or file that you send from one of your devices to another, end-to-end encrypted. It is a transfer tool, not a storage service: we don't keep what you send, and anything not received within a few minutes is gone for good. Keep your own copy of anything you need.

Your responsibilities

  • Use Postern only with devices you own or trust. Encryption can't protect what you type on, or receive on, a device that has malware or that someone else controls.
  • Before using what arrives, check that the 4-digit code on the receiving screen matches the one on your phone. If it doesn't, discard it.
  • Make sure the field you fill is the one you intend, on the website you intend.
  • Keep your passwords and other secrets safe. You're responsible for what you send and where you send it.
